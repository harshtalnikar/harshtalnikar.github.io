Features

Image & text import

AI-assisted extraction turns messy plans into a structured timer.

Voice guidance

Choose spoken, beep or silent countdowns. Keep your eyes off the screen.

Timed and rep-based

Planks on a clock, squats by the rep, with weight logging.

Rounds & rest

Circuits repeat with their own rest between rounds.

Free to use

Ad-supported, with a one-time Remove Ads purchase.

Build your own

Create and edit workouts manually whenever you like.
