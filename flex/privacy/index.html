Flex for Android

Privacy policy

Effective 23 October 2026

Flex is a workout timer app made by Harsh Talnikar, an individual developer based in India (“I”, “me”). This policy explains what data the app and its server handle. Questions: harshtalnikar@gmail.com.

Short version: there are no accounts and no analytics. Your workouts live on your phone. The text or photo you import is sent to an AI service to read it, and is not saved on my server. A hashed device identifier is stored to count imports. Ads are shown by Google AdMob.

1. Workouts you import

When you import a workout, the text you typed or the photo you chose is sent over HTTPS to my server (hosted on Render). The server forwards it to Google’s Gemini API, which reads it and returns the exercises. My server holds the content in memory only for the length of the request and does not save it. Photos are compressed before sending. The Android photo picker may leave a copy in the app’s temporary cache on your device.

Google processes what it receives under its own terms for the Gemini API, which may allow it to retain or use the content in line with those terms. Please don’t import photos or text containing personal or sensitive information.

2. Import limit and device identifier

To limit imports per month, the app reads your Android ID and sends it with import and usage requests. The server immediately converts it with a keyed cryptographic hash (HMAC-SHA256) and stores only the hash, the date of your first import in the current period, a count of successful imports, and any bonus imports you have earned (see section 3), in a Postgres database hosted by Neon in Singapore. The raw Android ID is not stored, and I can’t reverse the hash back to your device. I also store a total daily import count that isn’t linked to anyone. This data is kept for as long as the service runs.

3. Ads and purchases

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, including full-screen video ads. AdMob collects data such as your advertising ID, device information and IP address, under Google’s Privacy Policy. Flex asks AdMob for non-personalized ads only. The ad SDK runs even if you bought Remove Ads, but ads are then not displayed.

If you run out of imports, you can choose to watch a rewarded video ad for extra imports. To confirm that you watched the ad, Google’s AdMob sends a signed confirmation message to my server containing a one-time code that the app received from my server. It does not contain your name, advertising ID or device ID.

The optional Remove Ads purchase is processed by Google Play. I never see your payment details. The app stores only a yes/no flag on your device to remember the purchase.

4. App updates

When the app starts, it checks Expo’s update service (u.expo.dev) for updates. That service receives the app version, platform and update identifiers, and sees your IP address.

5. Server logs

My hosting provider keeps standard request logs (such as time, request path, status and user agent), and can see IP addresses at its network edge. My own code logs only error messages and never the content you import, your device ID or your hash.

6. Data that stays on your device

Your workouts, history, rep and weight logs, and settings are stored locally on your phone and are not sent to me. Depending on your phone’s backup settings, Android may include app data in your device backup to your Google account.

7. What I don’t do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools.
  • I don’t sell your data or use it for advertising profiles.

8. Your choices and deleting data

  • On your device: delete any workout or history entry in the app. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling removes all local data.
  • On my server: I can’t tell which stored hash is yours, so I can’t look up your record. If you have a privacy question or request, email me and I will do what I can.
  • Ads: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

9. Where data is processed

Google, Render, Neon and Expo operate in several countries, including Singapore and the United States, so your data may be processed outside your country.

10. Children

Flex is not directed to children under 13, and I don’t knowingly collect their personal data.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, I’ll update it here and change the date above.
